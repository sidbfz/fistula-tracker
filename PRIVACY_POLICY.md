# Privacy Policy for Fistula Tracker

**Last updated: September 2, 2026**

Fistula Tracker is a private recovery-record and reminder app developed by Siddharth Farkade. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app handles, where it is stored, and when third-party services may process limited information.

## Summary

Your recovery information is stored locally on your device. Fistula Tracker does not require an account and does not operate a server that receives your recovery records. The app does not include advertising or behavioral analytics, and the developer does not sell personal information.

If you use the optional one-time support purchase, Google Play and RevenueCat process limited technical and transaction information needed to provide, validate, and restore that purchase.

## Information You Choose to Store

You may choose to enter information such as:

- A name or nickname;
- Recovery stage and surgery or procedure details;
- Symptom and daily check-in records;
- Medicine names, schedules, and medication events;
- Recovery routines and reminders;
- Journal entries and recovery goals; and
- Wound or recovery photographs.

This information may include personal and sensitive health information. Optional fields can be left blank.

## How Recovery Information Is Used

The app uses the information you enter to provide features you choose, including displaying your recovery history, organizing medicines and routines, scheduling local reminders, keeping journal entries, and displaying your private photo timeline.

Fistula Tracker does not use recovery information to diagnose a condition, assess a wound, recommend treatment or medication doses, advertise to you, profile you, or train artificial-intelligence models.

## Local Storage and Sharing

Structured recovery records are stored in a local SQLite database. Photographs selected or captured through the app are copied into the app's private document storage.

The developer does not receive or have access to your recovery records. Fistula Tracker does not send your name, medical history, procedure details, medicines, routines, check-ins, journal entries, reminder content, recovery goals, or photographs to RevenueCat, advertisers, analytics providers, data brokers, healthcare providers, or a developer-operated server.

Your operating system or a backup service that you enable may include app data in a device backup. Such backups are controlled by you and the applicable platform or backup provider.

## Camera and Photo Library

Camera or photo-library permission is requested only when you choose to take or select a recovery photograph. The selected image is copied into private app storage. The app does not scan the rest of your photo library or upload selected photographs to a developer-operated server.

## Notifications

If you enable reminders, Fistula Tracker asks the operating system to schedule notifications using the times and wording you select. These are local, on-device notifications. You can disable individual reminders inside the app or disable all notifications through your device settings.

Medicine, routine, and check-in reminders are organizational tools only and do not constitute medical advice.

## Device Authentication

If you enable App Lock, authentication is performed by your device. Fistula Tracker receives only whether authentication succeeded. The app does not receive or store your fingerprint, facial image, passcode, or other biometric information.

## Google Play and RevenueCat

Fistula Tracker may offer an optional one-time support purchase. Google Play processes the payment and maintains the store transaction. RevenueCat is used to retrieve product availability, validate the transaction, restore purchases, and maintain purchase entitlement status.

For these purposes, RevenueCat may process an anonymous app-user identifier, device type, operating system, app and store information, last-seen time, product and purchase history, Google purchase token, transaction status, and entitlement status. Fistula Tracker does not intentionally attach recovery records or other health information to this purchase data.

These services process information under their own privacy policies:

- [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- [RevenueCat Privacy Policy](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy)

The optional purchase does not unlock medically relevant recovery features.

## Data Retention and Deletion

Recovery information remains on your device until you edit or delete it, clear the app's storage, or uninstall the app, subject to device backups that you control.

Fistula Tracker provides settings for deleting recovery history, private photographs, or all locally stored app data. Deleting local app data does not automatically delete transaction records retained by Google Play or RevenueCat. To request assistance concerning RevenueCat purchase data, contact the developer using the email address below.

## Security

Fistula Tracker relies on the security protections provided by your device and operating system, including app-private storage and optional device authentication. No storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Keep your device updated and protected with a passcode.

## Children

Fistula Tracker is not specifically directed to children. The developer does not knowingly collect children's recovery information through a developer-operated service. A parent or guardian should supervise use where required by applicable law.

## Medical and Emergency Disclaimer

Fistula Tracker is a personal record and reminder tool, not a medical device or healthcare service. It does not diagnose conditions, detect infection, prescribe care, recommend medication doses, or replace a qualified healthcare professional. Do not rely on the app during an emergency; contact local emergency services or an appropriate clinician.

## Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when the app's features or privacy practices change. The updated policy will show a revised “Last updated” date. Material changes will be reflected in the app and on the publicly available policy page as appropriate.
